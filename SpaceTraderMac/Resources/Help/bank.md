# Bank

The Bank (⌘5) lends money and sells insurance.

- You can borrow more when your police record is clean and your worth is high. Interest is charged every day you travel.
- With a very large debt you can't leave the system until you repay some of it.
- **Insurance** pays out the value of your ship when it is destroyed and you escape in a pod. Every day without a claim lowers the premium.
- The page also lists what your next warp will cost.
