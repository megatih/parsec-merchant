# Getting Started

You are a commander with a small ship, 1,000 credits and a whole galaxy to trade in. Buy goods where they are cheap, fly to a system where they are expensive, and sell them for a profit. With the money you earn you can buy better ships, weapons, shields and crew, and take on the quests you hear about on the way.

## The window

- The **dock bar** at the top opens the pages: Bridge, Shipyard, Equipment, Personnel, Bank, Commander Status, Quests, Galactic Chart, Logbook and Options. Each button shows its key (⌘1 to ⌘9, ⌥⌘O).
- The **status bar** below it always shows where you are, the day, your cash and debt, your cargo bays, fuel, hull and shields, and the latest event.
- The **Bridge** is home. Buying, selling, refuelling, repairs, choosing a target and warping all happen there, so a whole trade run needs no other page. **Esc** returns to the Bridge from any page.

## Your first trip

- Choose **File › Quick Start** for a Modern game at Normal difficulty, or **File › New Game…** to pick your own name, difficulty, ruleset and skills.
- In the **Cargo** panel of the Bridge, find a good that is cheap here and click **Max** in its row to buy as much as you can afford and carry.
- In the **Short-Range Chart**, click a system inside the range circle. It becomes your target, and the Cargo panel shows what your goods should sell for there.
- Click **Warp** in the Target System panel.
- When you arrive, click **Sell All Cargo**. Repeat, and keep an eye on your fuel.

Everything can be done with the mouse, a trackpad or the keyboard. Every action also has a menu item and a shortcut (see Keyboard Shortcuts): for example B buys, S sells, ⌘] picks the next target and ⌘J warps.

## Saving

The game autosaves when you arrive somewhere and when you quit. Use **File › Save** (⌘S) to keep a game you want to come back to, and **File › Open Saved Game…** (⌘O) to load it.
