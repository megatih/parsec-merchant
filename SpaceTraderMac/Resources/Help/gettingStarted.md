# Getting Started

You are a commander with a small ship, 1,000 credits and a whole galaxy to trade in. Buy goods where they are cheap, fly to a system where they are expensive, and sell them for a profit. With the money you earn you can buy better ships, weapons, shields and crew, and take on the quests you hear about on the way.

## Your first trip

- Choose **File › Quick Start** for a Modern game at Normal difficulty, or **File › New Game…** to pick your own name, difficulty, ruleset and skills.
- Open the **Market** (⌘2) and buy something that is cheap here.
- Go back to the **Bridge** (⌘1), pick a target system in range with **Next Target** (⌘]) and press **Warp** (⌘J).
- When you arrive, sell your cargo in the Market. Repeat, and keep an eye on your fuel.

## Saving

The game autosaves when you arrive somewhere and when you quit. Use **File › Save** (⌘S) to keep a game you want to come back to, and **File › Open Saved Game…** (⌘O) to load it.
