# Quests

The Quests page (⌘7) lists the open quests: what you have agreed to do, where to go and how many days are left when the clock is running. **Show on Galactic Chart** points the chart at a quest's system.

Quests start at special events on the Bridge. Some pay well, some change the galaxy, and some end badly if you take too long.

**Commander Status** (⌘6) shows your commander, skills, police record, reputation, finances and ship in one place.
