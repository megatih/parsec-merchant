# Travel

Warping uses fuel: one unit per parsec. A wormhole takes you to a distant system for a tax, without using fuel.

- The **Short-Range Chart** on the Bridge is the quick way: click a system in range, then **Warp** (⌘J).
- The **Galactic Chart** (⌘8) shows the whole galaxy. Click a system to select it; the **Selected System** panel beside the chart shows its facts and known prices, with **Set as Target**, **Track**, **Plot Route** and, at a wormhole, **Use Wormhole**. Double-click to target a system at once. Right-click for the same commands.
- Scroll with two fingers or drag to pan; pinch, turn the mouse wheel or use the zoom buttons to zoom. **Fit Range** (⌘0) shows the systems you can reach.
- The filters dim the systems that do not match, and the heatmap tints the chart by what you know.
- **Find…** (⌘F) looks a system up by name. The chart also opens in its own window (⇧⌘M).
- Every warp costs a day, and mercenaries, insurance and interest are paid on departure.
- On the way you may meet pirates, police, traders and stranger things.
- Arriving autosaves the game.
