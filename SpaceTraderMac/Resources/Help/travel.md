# Travel

Warping uses fuel: one unit per parsec. A wormhole takes you to a distant system for a tax, without using fuel.

- On the **Galaxy** chart, click a system to make it your target; its card has **Track**, **Plot Route** and, at a wormhole, **Use Wormhole**. Double-click to target it and move to the Warp button. Right-click (or touch and hold) for the same commands.
- Scroll with two fingers or drag to pan; pinch, turn the mouse wheel or use the zoom buttons to zoom.
- Every warp costs a day, and mercenaries, insurance and interest are paid on departure.
- On the way you may meet pirates, police, traders and stranger things.
- Arriving autosaves the game.
