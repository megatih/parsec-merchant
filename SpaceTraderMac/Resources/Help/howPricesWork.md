# How Prices Work

Each system's prices start from a base price for every good and change with:

- the system's **tech level**: goods are cheaper where they are produced, and some goods can't be bought or sold at low tech levels;
- the system's **government** and **size**;
- its **special resources**, such as rich soil or mineral riches, which make some goods cheap;
- **events** such as war, plague or drought, which make some goods very expensive for a while;
- a little random variation.

Your trader skill lowers buy prices. Selling is always at the local sell price. The best trades buy where a good is produced and sell where an event drives its price up.
