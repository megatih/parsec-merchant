# Encounters

When you meet another ship you choose what to do: attack, flee, surrender, ignore, bribe, submit to an inspection, trade or plunder, depending on who it is.

- The two ships are drawn side by side with their hull and shield bars. A badge names the other ship's role: Police, Pirate, Trader, Alien or Special.
- Each action is a button that shows its key (A to attack, F to flee, and so on). The same actions are in the Encounter menu. The combat log below the buttons records every round.
- Police inspect your cargo for illegal goods. Fleeing or attacking them hurts your police record.
- Pirates attack; fleeing works better with a good pilot, fighting with a good fighter.
- Traders may offer to trade in orbit.
- Attacking innocent ships makes you a criminal; destroying pirates raises your reputation.
