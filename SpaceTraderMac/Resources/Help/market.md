# Market

The Market (⌘2) lists the ten trade goods with the local buy and sell prices, what you carry and what you paid for it.

- Each row has quantity chips: **1**, **5**, **20** and **Max**. Pick one, then click **Buy** or **Sell** to trade that many. Max trades as much as you can afford, carry or hold.
- To trade an exact amount, type it in the row's field and click Buy or Sell.
- Right-click a row (or touch and hold it) for all of its actions, including Jettison or Dump when you carry the item.
- From the keyboard: select a row and press **B** to buy one unit or **S** to sell one unit; **⇧B** buys the most and **⇧S** sells everything. Holding ⇧, ⌘, ⇧⌘ or ⌥ while clicking Buy or Sell trades 5, 20, 100 or all.
- Goods the system doesn't trade show no price. Illegal goods (firearms and narcotics) can get you in trouble with the police.
- Your free cargo bays limit what you can buy. The Options let you keep some bays empty for goods found on the way.
- Use **Find…** (⌘F) to filter the list.
