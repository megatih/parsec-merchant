# Cargo and Trading

The Cargo panel of the Bridge lists the ten trade goods. For each good it shows what you hold, what you paid per unit, the local sell and buy prices, the stock, the price at your target system and the profit per unit there.

- Type a number in a row's **Quantity** field and click **Buy** or **Sell**. An empty field means 1.
- **Max** buys as many as your cash and free bays allow. **All** sells every unit you hold.
- **Sell All Cargo** (⌥⌘S) sells everything in the hold that can be sold here.
- From the keyboard: move through the rows with ↑ and ↓, then press **B** to buy, **S** to sell, **⇧B** to buy the maximum and **⇧S** to sell all. Typing digits sets the row's quantity.
- Where a good cannot be sold, Sell becomes **Dump**: you pay to have it taken away.
- A star marks the best buys for your target. Right-click a row for **Price Details…** (⌥⌘I), which explains why the price is what it is.
- Goods the system doesn't trade show a dash. Illegal goods (firearms and narcotics) are marked, and can get you in trouble with the police.

The target's prices are estimates or prices you remember from an earlier visit, shown with their age. They carry no guarantee. Options let you keep some bays empty and some cash in reserve.
