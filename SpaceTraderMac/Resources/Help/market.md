# Market

The Market (⌘2) lists the ten trade goods with the local buy and sell prices, what you carry and what you paid for it.

- Select a row and press **B** to buy one unit or **S** to sell one unit; Buy Max and Sell All trade as much as you can.
- Goods the system doesn't trade show no price. Illegal goods (firearms and narcotics) can get you in trouble with the police.
- Your free cargo bays limit what you can buy. The Options let you keep some bays empty for goods found on the way.
- Use **Find…** (⌘F) to filter the list.
