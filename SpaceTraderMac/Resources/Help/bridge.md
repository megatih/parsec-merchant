# Bridge

The Bridge (⌘1) shows the system you are docked at: its size, tech level, government, resources and police and pirate activity. It is also where you choose your next destination.

- **Target**: the system you will warp to. Cycle through the systems in range with ‹ and › (⌘[ and ⌘]) or use the target picker in the toolbar.
- **Warp** (⌘J) flies to the target. Warp is disabled when the target is out of range or you can't pay mercenaries, insurance or wormhole tax; the help tag says why.
- **Fuel and repairs**: Fill Fuel (⇧⌘F) and Repair Hull (⇧⌘R) buy a full tank or a full hull repair, as much as your cash allows.
- **Special events**: when a system has something for you, the Bridge shows it, and the sidebar marks Bridge with a badge.
- **Track** a system to see its distance and to come back to it later.
