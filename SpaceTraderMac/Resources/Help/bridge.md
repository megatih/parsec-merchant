# Bridge

The Bridge (⌘1) is the one screen you need for trading. It has four panels, and all four are always on screen.

- **System Information** shows the system you are docked at: its size, tech level, government, resources, and police and pirate activity. It holds the fuel and hull gauges with **Fill Tank** (⇧⌘F) and **Repair Hull** (⇧⌘R), and the buttons for the newspaper, a special event, mercenaries for hire and open quests.
- **Cargo** is the market: one row for each of the ten goods (see Cargo and Trading).
- **Short-Range Chart** shows the systems around you and the range of your fuel. Click a system to make it your target. **Galactic Chart** opens the whole galaxy.
- **Target System** shows the system you will warp to: its facts, what the trip will cost, and the fuel you will have left. Step through the systems in range with ‹ and › (⌘[ and ⌘]), **Track** a system to keep its distance in view, and **Find…** (⌘F) a system by name.

**Warp** (⌘J) flies to the target. It is disabled when the target is out of range or you can't pay mercenaries, insurance, interest or wormhole tax; its help tag says why.

After a trip the status bar shows a summary of the arrival. Click it to read the full report.
