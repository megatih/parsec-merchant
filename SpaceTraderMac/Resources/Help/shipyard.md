# Shipyard

The Shipyard (⌘3) sells ships when the system's tech level is high enough. Your old ship is traded in, and its value is subtracted from the price.

- Each ship's picture is drawn from its statistics: freighters carry containers, warships show their gun mounts.
- Click **Compare** on two or more ships to see them side by side; double-click a ship, or click **Buy…**, to review the purchase.
- Compare ships by cargo bays, weapon, shield and gadget slots, crew quarters, fuel range and hull strength.
- Special equipment such as a unique laser or shield can sometimes be moved to the new ship for a fee; anything that doesn't fit is lost.
- An **escape pod** saves you (but not your ship) when you are destroyed.
- In rulesets with custom shipyards, the **Designer** builds a ship to your own specification.
