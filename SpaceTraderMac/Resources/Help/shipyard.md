# Shipyard

The Shipyard (⌘2) sells ships when the system's tech level is high enough. Your old ship is traded in, and its value is subtracted from the price.

- Select a ship to see it drawn, with its cargo bays, weapon, shield and gadget slots, crew quarters, fuel range and hull strength compared with your own ship's.
- Click **Buy Ship…** to review the purchase. Special equipment such as a unique laser or shield can sometimes be moved to the new ship for a fee; anything that doesn't fit is lost.
- An **escape pod** saves you (but not your ship) when you are destroyed.
- In rulesets with custom shipyards, the **Designer** builds a ship to your own specification.

Where no ships are sold, the page says so and still offers what the system has.
