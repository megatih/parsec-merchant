# Personnel

Mercenaries (⌘4) improve your ship's skills: pilot, fighter, trader and engineer. Your ship uses the best value of anyone on board.

- The page shows your crew and the mercenaries for hire here, each with a portrait and skills.
- Each mercenary needs a free crew quarter and is paid a daily wage when you warp.
- **Hire** and **Fire** mercenaries while docked. The Personnel button in the dock bar shows how many are for hire here.
