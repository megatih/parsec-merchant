# Equipment

The Equipment page (⌘3) buys and sells weapons, shields and gadgets. What is offered depends on the system's tech level.

- The page shows what is installed in your ship's slots and what is for sale here. Click **Buy** on an item for sale, or **Sell** on an installed one.
- Each item needs a free slot of its kind on your ship. Selling returns three quarters of the price.
- Extra cargo bays can't be sold while the bays are in use.
- Better weapons win fights faster; better shields absorb damage before it reaches the hull.
