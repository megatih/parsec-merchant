# Outfitter

The Outfitter (⌘4) buys and sells weapons, shields and gadgets. What is offered depends on the system's tech level.

- Click **Buy** on an item, or drag it onto a free slot of its kind. To sell, click the slot's **Sell** button or drag the installed item onto the sell well.
- Each item needs a free slot of its kind on your ship. Selling returns three quarters of the price.
- Extra cargo bays can't be sold while the bays are in use.
- Better weapons win fights faster; better shields absorb damage before it reaches the hull.
