# UI-POINTER: Pointer and touch first, keyboard complete

> Format (PRD §7): `- **ID** {Mn} [UI] [M][W][P] statement (source)`. `[M]` Modern, `[W]` Windows 2.01, `[P]` Palm 1.2.2. `[UI]` = verified by app tests (`SpaceTraderMacTests`, hosted in the app) or XCUITest (`SpaceTraderMacUITests`). Bullets without `[UI]` are pure functions of the app target, unit-tested in `SpaceTraderMacTests`. `[manual]` = a `docs/QA-REPORT.md` row.
> Milestone: everything here is {M16} (`docs/PRD-1.1.md` §6 M16).
> Normative inputs: `docs/PRD-1.1.md` §3 (Apple HIG as updated on 28 October 2026 for Touch on Mac), §4 D9, §6 M16; UX-VISION §1.4 ("Pointer and touch first, keyboard complete"), §4.2, §4.3, §4.4, §4.7, §10.
> **Ownership (do not restate, reference).** Keyboard shortcuts and the binding table: UI-KEYS (unchanged by this work). Market trade commands and clamping: UI-SHELL-062…064. Map hit testing and actions: MAP-014, MAP-019, MAP-020, MAP-044, MAP-048…053. Encounter actions: UI-ENC-027. Accessibility of every control: A11Y.
> SDK check (2026-11-18, macOS 27.2 SDK): the reported names `NSIsTouchNative` (Info.plist) and `directTouchSystemGesturesDisabled()` (SwiftUI) do not occur in the SDK's headers or `.swiftinterface` files, so neither is adopted; the app relies on the standard gestures, which the system maps to touch.

## 1. Principle

- **UI-PTR-001** {M16} [M][W][P] Every action in the game is discoverable and quick with a pointer or a finger alone: nothing needs a modifier key, a hovering pointer or the keyboard. The keyboard remains a complete second path: `KeyBindingTable`, the menus and every shortcut are unchanged (UI-KEYS-003), and modifier clicks remain as accelerators of visible controls. Key hints live in menus, help tags and the Help page, not in primary UI. Density is unchanged: controls did not grow, their targets did. (source: PRD-1.1 §3, §4 D9; HIG "Don't use touch as the only way…", "Keep the layout and size of interface elements the same when you support touch")

## 2. Targets and feedback

- **UI-PTR-002** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **36 pt targets.** `pointerTarget(minimum: 36)` (`SpaceTraderMac/Accessibility/PointerTarget.swift`) gives a custom interactive element a real frame of at least 36 pt on each axis, with a matching content shape, while its visible chrome keeps its size inside that frame (the frame, not only the hit shape, grows, because XCUITest and assistive tools measure the accessibility frame). Standard push buttons get the same through `PointerButtonStyle`, the push-button style of every scene and sheet root: the system's button keeps its regular bezel and layout height, and a 36 pt target centred on it is the element people and tools press (a tap anywhere in it triggers the button). `PointerTarget.minimum == 36`. `PointerTargetUITests` walks `UISurfaceCatalog`: every hittable button-type element with an app identifier measures at least 36 pt on its short side. Exempt by element type are the standard controls the system already expands for touch: steppers, sliders, checkboxes, pop-up buttons, menu items, table rows and cells, toolbar items, segmented controls, window controls and the buttons of the system's confirmation dialog. (source: PRD-1.1 §3 "36 pt per axis", §4 D9; HIG "Make tap targets bigger, not the controls themselves")
- **UI-PTR-003** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Hover feedback.** `hoverHighlight()` gives a tappable card or row a visible reaction to the pointer before the click (a tinted rounded background that appears while the pointer is over it, instantly under Reduce Motion), and never changes layout. Every view that takes `.onTapGesture` carries `hoverHighlight` (source audit); that includes the 36 pt target of `PointerButtonStyle`, whose tint stays the size of the button's bezel. It is feedback only: nothing is revealed or enabled by hover alone (UI-PTR-004). (source: PRD-1.1 §2 "No hover feedback anywhere", §4 D9)
- **UI-PTR-004** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **No modifier-only, hover-only or keyboard-only action.** Source audit (`SourceAuditTests`): no `.controlSize(.small)` or `.mini` on a button outside an allowlist of documented exceptions; no action is reachable only through `NSEvent.modifierFlags` or a modifier-qualified tap (each such accelerator sits next to a visible control that sends the same command, named in the audit's table); every hover-revealed surface has a click or tap that shows the same content (UI-PTR-012). (source: PRD-1.1 §3 "provide touch equivalents for anything that needs a modifier key or a hovering pointer")
- **UI-PTR-005** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Context menus on every entity** mirror the visible actions, so touch-and-hold and right-click reach them: Market rows (Buy 1/5/20/Max, Sell 1/5/20/All, Jettison or Dump, Why This Price), Shipyard cards (Buy…, Compare / Remove from Compare), Outfitter items and slots (Buy, Sell), crew rows (Hire, Fire), quest rows (Show on Map where the quest has a location), the Bridge target card and map systems (Set Target, Track, Plot Route, Use Wormhole; MAP-053). A menu item that would be rejected is disabled with the reason. A context menu never holds an action that has no visible control. (source: PRD-1.1 §6 M16; HIG "touch and hold opens the context menu")
- **UI-PTR-006** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Double-click or double-tap is the primary action of a card:** a Shipyard card opens its purchase sheet; a map system sets the target and moves focus to Warp (MAP-051); an Outfitter item for sale opens its buy confirmation. It never does anything the single click plus the visible button cannot do. (source: PRD-1.1 §3 gestures "double tap opens")

## 3. Sections

- **UI-PTR-007** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Market chips.** Each Market row has quantity chips 1, 5, 20 and Max (`market.row.<item>.chip.<1|5|20|max>`, one selected, 1 by default, remembered per window) and explicit **Buy** (`market.row.<item>.buy`) and **Sell** (`market.row.<item>.sell`) buttons that trade the selected chip's quantity, clamped as in UI-SHELL-062, so a common trade takes no more clicks than before (Max then Buy replaces Buy Max). The typed amount has a visible submit: when the quantity field (`market.row.<item>.quantity`) between them is not empty, the same **Sell** and **Buy** buttons trade the typed value instead of the chip's. Jettison or Dump is a visible button (`market.row.<item>.menu.disposal`) whenever jettisoning or dumping the item is possible, and an item of the row's context menu. The modifier multipliers stay as accelerators of the same Buy and Sell buttons. (source: PRD-1.1 §2 "Market", §6 M16)
- **UI-PTR-008** {M16} [M][W][P] `TradeChip` (`one, five, twenty, max`) is pure: `quantity(maximum:)` is 1, 5, 20 or `maximum`, clamped to `0...maximum`; `TradeQuantity.resolve(chip:modifiers:maximum:)` returns the modifier quantity when a modifier is held and the chip's otherwise. Unit-tested for every chip × modifier combination. (source: UI-SHELL-063)
- **UI-PTR-009** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Shipyard.** Each card has a visible **Compare** toggle (`shipyard.ship.<ShipType>.compare`) that adds it to or removes it from the comparison (⌘-click remains an accelerator); double-click opens the purchase sheet (UI-PTR-006); the Buy button stays. (source: PRD-1.1 §2 "Shipyard compare needs ⌘-click")
- **UI-PTR-010** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Outfitter drag and drop.** An item for sale is `.draggable` and each free slot of its type is a `.dropDestination` that asks to buy it; an installed item is draggable onto the sell well (`outfitter.sellWell`), which asks to sell it. The confirmations are the ones the buttons use (ALR-080/ALR-090). The Buy and Sell buttons remain as the tap path, and the payload (`OutfitterDragItem`, a `Transferable` value) and the drop rules (`OutfitterDrop.action(for:onto:)`) are pure and unit-tested: a weapon dropped on a shield slot does nothing; a full section rejects the drop with the buy check's reason. (source: UX-VISION §4.4; UI-SHELL-078; HIG "touch and hold then drag")
- **UI-PTR-011** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Map selection card.** A click or tap on a system or wormhole pins a selection card (`<scope>.selectionCard`) with the hover card's facts and the buttons **Set Target** (`<scope>.selection.setTarget`), **Track** / **Stop Tracking** (`<scope>.selection.track`), **Plot Route** (`<scope>.selection.plotRoute`) and, on a wormhole, **Use Wormhole** (`<scope>.selection.useWormhole`), each sending the MAP-048…053 command. The card takes hit testing, stays until a click on empty space, Esc, a pan, a zoom or a warp (its own commands, which change the state, keep it open), and is placed like the hover card (MAP-044). Hovering still previews without pinning. The click also sets the target as before (MAP-049/050), so the pointer path costs no extra click. The Bridge mini chart has no card: a tap on a system there still sets the target, and a tap on its empty space or on its Open Galaxy Chart button (`bridge.miniChart.open`) opens the Galaxy section. (source: PRD-1.1 §2 "the hover card cannot be clicked", §6 M16)
- **UI-PTR-012** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Map gestures.** A plain mouse wheel zooms by ×1.1 per step about the pointer; two-finger trackpad scrolling pans; pinch zooms; a one-finger or primary-button drag pans (MAP-014). `MapScroll.action(deltaY:precise:)` is pure: precise (trackpad) deltas pan, wheel steps zoom. Everything a hover shows is also shown by a click (UI-PTR-011), and tracking, routing and wormhole use need no modifier (the ⌥-click accelerator remains). (source: PRD-1.1 §2 "Map wheel-zoom needs ⌘; tracking needs ⌥-click"; HIG gestures)
- **UI-PTR-013** {M16} [M][W][P] **Map targets.** Hit radii are at least 18 pt for systems and wormholes (MAP-020), so a glyph is a 36 pt target; where two glyphs are closer than that, the nearer centre wins (MAP-019). The zoom and fit HUD buttons and the search suggestion rows are 36 pt targets. (source: PRD-1.1 §2 "glyph hit areas go down to about 16 pt")
- **UI-PTR-014** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Encounter.** The action buttons are at least 36 pt high (`pointerTarget()` in the label, so the bar still fits its actions at the largest Text Size) and show the action name only; when the view scrolls at a large Text Size the action bar stays on screen below the scrolling part; the key is in the help tag ("Attack (A)", UI-ENC-027). The Plunder, Trade in Orbit and Make Room sheets have quantity chips (1, 5, All) beside their steppers (a Plunder row shows its chips while something can be taken). (source: PRD-1.1 §6 M16)
- **UI-PTR-015** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Elsewhere.** Sidebar rows show the section name and badge only; the ⌘N hint stays in the View menu (UI-SHELL-035). The tribble buttons, the New Game wizard's − and + buttons and the tip close buttons are 36 pt targets. The Bank's loan sliders step by `BankSlider.step(range:)` = the range divided into at most 100 steps, rounded to 1, 10, 100 or 1,000 cr., so a drag reaches every useful value. (source: PRD-1.1 §2 "Small targets", §6 M16)

## 4. Touch

- **UI-PTR-016** {M16} [M][W][P] **Gestures** are the system's: tap selects, double tap opens (UI-PTR-006), a one-finger slide scrolls lists and pans the map, pinch zooms the map, touch and hold opens the context menu (UI-PTR-005), touch and hold then drag drags (UI-PTR-010). The app registers no gesture that needs more than one finger other than pinch, and none that conflicts with these. No touch-only control exists: every function is available with the keyboard and pointing device. (source: PRD-1.1 §3; HIG "Don't use touch as the only way to access controls or features")
- **UI-PTR-017** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] **Touch equivalents for hover.** Touch has no hover, so: the map's hover card has the pinned selection card (UI-PTR-011); a disabled control's reason, shown in its help tag, is also its accessibility hint and, where the control has a row, the row's inline status text; the encounter keys in help tags are also on the Help page. (source: HIG "Touch has no hover: reveal hidden controls on tap and offer them in a context menu")
- **UI-PTR-018** {M16} [M][W][P] [manual] Touch checklist in QA-REPORT (hands-on part in `docs/QA-HANDCHECKS.md` when no touch hardware is attached): on a touch-display Mac or an iPad through Sidecar, a new game can be played to the first sale by touch alone: chips and Buy, tap a system and Set Target, Warp, an encounter by its buttons, Sell; touch and hold opens the row and map menus; pinch zooms and a slide pans the map; no control is too small to hit. (source: PRD-1.1 §6 M16 exit checks)

## 5. Scenario tests

- **UI-PTR-019** {M16} [UI] [M][W][P] `PointerOnlyScenarioUITests`: new game → buy cargo with chips → pick a target on the map → warp → resolve an encounter → sell, using clicks only: no `typeKey` and no modifier after launch. (source: PRD-1.1 §6 M16 tests)
