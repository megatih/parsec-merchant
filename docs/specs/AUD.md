## Conventions (normative for every bullet below)

- **Procedural only.** Every sound is synthesised at run time from oscillators, noise, envelopes and filters in `ParsecAudio`. No sample files of any format are shipped or loaded, and no `AVAudioFile`/`AVAudioPlayerNode` with file content is used (PRD §6.6 "No sample files").
- **Real-time safety.** Code reachable from the render callback is `nonisolated`, does not allocate, does not take locks, does not call Objective-C or Swift runtime APIs that may allocate (`print`, `String`, `Array` growth, closures that capture, existential boxing, `Task`, `DispatchQueue`, `await`, logging), and touches no Swift global or `static let` for the first time (all tables are materialised in `SynthCore.init`). It communicates with the main actor only through preallocated lock-free structures built on `Synchronization.Atomic` (research 05 §3).
- **No game state.** `ParsecAudio` imports `AVFoundation`, `Synchronization` and Foundation only; it does **not** import `ParsecEngine`. The app maps engine events to `SoundCue` requests in `SpaceTraderMac/Audio/` with exhaustive `switch`es and no `default`.
- **Never the only signal.** Every cue and every haptic has a visual twin that is shown whether or not sound is on (PRD §6.6, UX-VISION §9). Audio and haptics never change game state, RNG draws or `cheatsUsed` (CHEAT-050, RNG-036).
- **Files.** Package: `Packages/ParsecKit/Sources/ParsecAudio/` with `Core/` (render path: `SynthCore`, voices, oscillators, filters, envelopes, ring buffer, music generator), `Cues/` (`SoundCue`, recipes), `Output/` (`AudioOutput`, the only file that touches `AVAudioEngine`). App: `SpaceTraderMac/Audio/` (`AudioService`, `AudioCueMap`, `CueScheduler`, `HapticsService`, `HapticMap`, `SoundSettings`) and `SpaceTraderMac/Views/Settings/SoundSettingsView.swift`. SPDX headers, Swift 6, zero warnings.

## 1. Synthesis core (ParsecAudio)

- **AUD-001** {M11} [M][W][P] `SynthCore` is a `nonisolated final class SynthCore: @unchecked Sendable` (the `@unchecked` is justified in a comment: all mutable state is either owned by the render thread or exchanged through `Atomic`s). `init(sampleRate: Double, sfxVoices: Int = 16, musicVoices: Int = 6, queueCapacity: Int = 64, seed: UInt64)` preallocates every buffer (voice states, delay lines, the request ring, scratch buffers of 4096 frames) with `UnsafeMutablePointer.allocate` and frees them in `deinit`. Sample rates from 22 050 to 192 000 Hz are supported; all recipe times are in seconds and all frequencies in Hz, converted with the instance's sample rate. (source: PRD §6 "procedural synthesis (nonisolated, allocation-free render)"; research 05 §3)
- **AUD-002** {M11} [M][W][P] `SynthCore.render(frameCount: Int, left: UnsafeMutablePointer<Float>, right: UnsafeMutablePointer<Float>)` is the single render entry point: it drains pending requests from the ring, mixes active SFX voices (with equal-power panning) and the music voices, applies the smoothed effects, music and master gains, runs a soft limiter, and writes `frameCount` non-interleaved Float32 samples per channel. `frameCount` up to 4096 is accepted; larger counts are processed in 4096-frame chunks. It is callable from any thread and is what both the `AVAudioSourceNode` block and the tests call. (source: PRD §6.6 "A test calls the render function off the main thread"; research 05 §7 "`AVAudioSourceNode` for synthesis")
- **AUD-003** {M11} [M][W][P] **Control path.** `SynthCore.enqueue(_ r: CueRequest) -> Bool` (main actor producer, render consumer) writes a POD `CueRequest {cue: SoundCue, variant: UInt8, pitchSemitones: Float, gain: Float, pan: Float (−1…1), delay: Float (s, 0…2)}` into a single-producer single-consumer ring of fixed capacity; when full it returns `false` and increments the atomic `droppedRequests` counter (never blocks). `setGains(master:effects:music:)`, `setMusic(enabled:mood:)` and `stopAll()` store into atomics read once per render call. `renderCount`, `droppedRequests` and `activeVoices` are atomic counters readable from the main actor. (source: research 05 §3 "must not allocate, and must not take locks")
- **AUD-004** {M11} [M][W][P] **Building blocks** (all value types with in-place `mutating` sample functions): band-limited saw and square (PolyBLEP), triangle, sine, two-operator FM, a white-noise source (xorshift32 seeded from `seed` per voice, so output is deterministic), state-variable filter (low/high/band-pass with modulated cutoff), ADSR and exponential-decay envelopes, exponential pitch sweep, LFO, a feedback delay (fixed preallocated 1 s line) and a small Schroeder reverb with preallocated lines for the warp and achievement tails. No `AVAudioUnitReverb` or other Audio Units are required. (source: research 05 §7 "laser = frequency-swept square or saw … explosion = filtered noise burst … warp = rising sweep plus reverb … FM bells … slow generative pads")
- **AUD-005** {M11} [M][W][P] **Voices and priority.** Each `SoundCue` has a priority: 3 = explosion, low-hull alarm, arrest siren, game-end fanfare; 2 = combat cues and encounter stingers; 1 = economy, travel, quest and notice cues; 0 = UI cues. When all 16 SFX voices are busy a new request steals the oldest voice of the lowest priority that is ≤ its own; otherwise it is dropped and counted. A stolen voice is released with a 5 ms fade, never cut (no clicks). (source: UX-VISION §8; research 05 §3)
- **AUD-006** {M11} [M][W][P] **Gain law and smoothing.** A slider value `v ∈ [0, 1]` maps to linear gain `v²`. Effective SFX gain = master² × effects² × request gain; effective music gain = master² × music². Every gain change (settings, mute, app inactive) ramps linearly over 30 ms inside the render loop, so no zipper noise or click. The soft limiter is `x / (1 + |x|)` scaled so that inputs up to 0.5 pass within 1 dB; output samples are always finite and in `[−1, 1]`. (source: UX-VISION §8 "master, effects and music volumes")
- **AUD-007** {M11} [M][W][P] **Offline rendering for tests** (not on the real-time path, allocation allowed): `SynthCore.renderOffline(requests: [CueRequest], seconds: Double) -> (left: [Float], right: [Float])` renders by calling `render` in 512-frame blocks. Equal inputs, seed and sample rate give bit-identical output. (source: PRD §8.2 determinism spirit; PRD §6.6 "A test calls the render function")

## 2. Output and lifecycle

- **AUD-008** {M11} [M][W][P] `AudioOutput` (ParsecAudio `Output/`, `nonisolated final class`, used by the app from the main actor) owns one `AVAudioEngine` and one `AVAudioSourceNode`. The source-node render block is created inside ParsecAudio by a `nonisolated` factory that captures only the `SynthCore` reference, so it carries no main-actor isolation (the app target's default `MainActor` isolation never applies to it; research 05 §3). The block converts the `AudioBufferList` to the two channel pointers and calls `SynthCore.render`; it returns `noErr` and sets `isSilence` when no voice is active and music is off. The app never writes a render closure. (source: CLAUDE.md "The app only wires it up"; PRD §11 "Strict concurrency (audio …) | `ParsecAudio` package target (nonisolated)")
- **AUD-009** {M11} [M][W][P] `AudioOutput.start() throws(AudioOutputError)` reads the output hardware format from `engine.outputNode.outputFormat(forBus: 0)`; when its sample rate is 0 or it has no channels it throws `.noOutputDevice` without touching the engine further. Otherwise it creates `SynthCore` at that sample rate, attaches the source node with a standard non-interleaved stereo Float32 format at that rate, connects it to `mainMixerNode`, calls `prepare()` and `start()`. Any thrown engine error becomes `.engineFailed(code: Int)`. `stop()` stops the engine and detaches the node. All engine calls happen on the main actor, never in the render block. (source: research 05 §7 "A sandboxed app needs no entitlement for output")
- **AUD-010** {M11} [M][W][P] Configuration changes (`AVAudioEngineConfigurationChange`, e.g. a device switch or sample-rate change) are observed on the main actor: the output stops, rebuilds `SynthCore` at the new rate (dropping in-flight voices, keeping gains and music settings) and restarts; three failed restarts in a row set the status to `.failed` and stop retrying until the next settings change or app activation. (source: research 05 §7)
- **AUD-011** {M11} [M][W][P] `AudioService.status` is one of `.offForLaunch` (`-PMAudio off`), `.idle` (not started yet or paused), `.running`, `.noOutputDevice`, `.failed(code)`. Failures are logged once per occurrence with `Logger(subsystem: <bundle id>, category: "audio")` at `.error` level, increment `errorCount`, never show an alert, and leave the game fully playable with the visual twins. (source: PRD §6.6; UX-VISION §1.2 "Never stack modal dialogs")
- **AUD-012** {M11} [M][W][P] **Power and inactivity.** The engine starts at launch when audio is on for this launch (not `-PMAudio off`) and Mute is off; a master volume of 0 keeps it running silently (the tests rely on this, AUD-050/051). When the app resigns active and "Mute when inactive" is on, gains ramp to zero and the engine pauses after 1 s; it resumes on activation. When Mute is on for more than 2 s the engine also pauses, and it resumes when Mute is turned off. While paused, cue requests are discarded (not queued for later). (source: UX-VISION §8 "Sound is muted when the app is inactive (optional)")

(source: UI-SHELL-001 services; SessionServices.swift stand-ins)

## 5. Generative music (optional, off by default)

- **AUD-037** {M11} [M][W][P] `MusicGenerator` (inside `SynthCore`, allocation-free) plays a slow pad on the 6 music voices: four detuned triangle/saw voices through a low-pass whose cutoff drifts with a 0.05 Hz LFO, plus one sparse bell voice that plays a pentatonic note every 4–12 s chosen by the core's seeded generator. Chords change every 8 s with a 2 s overlap. With the same seed, mood sequence and sample rate the output is bit-identical (test). (source: PRD §6.6 "Optional generative music"; UX-VISION §8 "an optional generative pad"; research 05 §7 "slow generative pads")
- **AUD-038** {M11} [M][W][P] `MusicMood` = `.calm` (Welcome and docked: major seventh progression I–vi–IV–V), `.travel` (transit: suspended/Lydian chords, slightly brighter filter), `.tension` (encounter: minor progression over a low 1 Hz pulse drone), `.silent` (game over: fade out). `GameSession` derives the mood from `DetailMode` (UI-ENC-001) and calls `setMood` on change; a mood change crossfades over 3 s. Unit test: `MusicMood.derive(detailMode:)` for every `DetailMode`. (source: UX-VISION §8 "shifts mood docked, in transit and in combat")
- **AUD-039** {M11} [M][W][P] Music is **off by default** (`settings.sound.musicEnabled = false`). When it is off the music voices are not rendered (no CPU), and turning it on starts with a 2 s fade-in. Music never ducks below SFX by more than its own slider: SFX and music only sum through the limiter. (source: PRD §6.6 "off by default")

## 6. Haptics
