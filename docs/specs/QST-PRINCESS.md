# QST-PRINCESS: The kidnapped Princess

> Format (PRD §7): `- **ID** {Mn} [M][W][P] statement (source)`. `{M4}` is C# 2.01 behaviour, kept in Modern. The chain (and the Scorpion) does not exist in Palm (RUL-013): no bullet here carries `[P]`.
> Sources: C# 2.01 at `/Users/megatih/Downloads/SpaceTraderSource201`, re-read on 2026-11-17. Shared rules are QST-INDEX §3. Placement: `Princess` at Galvon (121), `PrincessCentauri` at Centauri (120), `PrincessInthara` at Inthara (122), `PrincessQonos` at Qonos (124) (GAL §10.1).
> Status constants: `QuestStatus.Princess` = notStarted 0, flyCentauri 1, flyInthara 2, flyQonos 3, rescued 4 (then counts while aboard), impatient 14, returned 15, done 16. "Aboard" = `CrewID.princess` (53) is in a crew slot (C# `Ship.PrincessOnBoard`, Ship.cs:1155-1161).

## 1. The search

- **QST-PRINCESS-001** {M4} [M][W] `Princess` (Galvon) is shown iff `rec >= 5 && rep >= 40` (no status test); it is message-only with price 0. Handling adds 1 to `princess` (0 → 1) and replaces Galvon's event with `PrincessReturned` (not removed), so the offer appears once. While the `Princess` event is in place, arriving at Galvon adds `NewsEvent.princess` (NEWS-034). (source: StarSystem.cs:230-233; Game.cs:2391-2395; Consts.cs:341; Game.cs:2782-2784)
- **QST-PRINCESS-002** {M4} [M][W] `PrincessCentauri` is shown iff `1 <= princess <= 3` and `PrincessInthara` iff `2 <= princess <= 3`; both are message-only with price 0, add 1 to `princess` and are removed. Inthara is therefore hidden until Centauri was handled. Arrivals add `princessCentauri` (status 1) and `princessInthara` (status 2) headlines (NEWS-034). (source: StarSystem.cs:234-241; Game.cs:2396-2399; Consts.cs:342-343; Game.cs:2785-2792)

## 2. The Scorpion

- **QST-PRINCESS-003** {M4} [M][W] With `princess == 3`, click 1 of every trip to Qonos is the fixed Scorpion encounter (ENC-010 (d), ENC-011): the opponent is `specialShips.scorpion` (ENC-012/014, 2 military lasers, 2 reflective shields), and its damage persists between encounters. Arriving at Qonos adds `princessQonos` (status 3) or `princessRescued` (status 4) (NEWS-034). (source: Game.cs:807-813, :2793-2798)
- **QST-PRINCESS-004** {M4} [M][W] Attacking the Scorpion needs disruptor power (`noDisruptors`, CMB-005). It cannot be destroyed: at hull 0 it is left with hull 1 and disabled (CMB-042). Disabling it sets `princess = 4`, adds a pirate kill, `rec += 1` and `rep += 9` (CMB-045, CMB-077) and emits `opponentDisabled`. Fleeing or escaping leaves the status at 3, so the encounter returns on the next trip to Qonos. (source: Game.cs:1381-1386, :1195-1226, :1600-1604)

## 3. Taking her aboard

- **QST-PRINCESS-005** {M4} [M][W] `PrincessQonos` is shown iff `princess == 4` and she is not aboard; it is a question with price 0. With no free crew quarter it is rejected with `CommandError.noFreeQuarters` (ALR-154) and nothing changes. Otherwise she is hired into the first free crew slot (C# `Ship.Hire`), the event is removed and the status stays 4; if her skills (4/3/8/9, CRW-020/024) raise the ship's effective Trader skill, buy prices are recalculated (QST-013). The app shows ALR-162 from `specialEventCompleted(.princessQonos)`. (source: StarSystem.cs:242-245; Game.cs:2400-2412; Consts.cs:344; Ship.cs:608-622)
- **QST-PRINCESS-006** {M4} [M][W] While aboard she is special crew: wage 0, cannot be fired (CRW-038), contributes her skills (CRW-020), needs a quarter in any new ship (`passengersNeedQuarters`, SHP-036), does not relocate, and is not illegal cargo (POL-051). (source: Consts.cs:282-295; Ship.cs:1131-1137)
- **QST-PRINCESS-007** {M4} [M][W] Impatience (TRV-080, `passengerImpatience` RUL-061): in each `IncDays` call while she is aboard, status 9 emits `GameEvent.passengerConcerned(crewID: CrewID.princess)` (ALR-157), else status 13 emits `.passengerImpatient(crewID: CrewID.princess)` (ALR-160) and sets her four skills to 0 for the rest of the game; then the status rises by 1 if below 14. The rise is 1 per call, whatever `n` (a 30-day jail term counts once). Impatience never ends the quest. (source: Game.cs:2650-2665)
- **QST-PRINCESS-008** {M4} [M][W] Surrendering to pirates with her aboard is refused with `CommandError.surrenderPrincess` (POL-077, ALR-058) unless hidden cargo bays are fitted; with them she is hidden (`preciousHidden`) and stays aboard. (source: Game.cs:1860-1880)

## 4. Failure paths

- **QST-PRINCESS-009** {M4} [M][W] Arrest with her aboard follows POL-093…095: with two or more ordinary mercenaries every crew slot is cleared in step (7), she leaves and her status is kept (the quest is stuck: no event can be shown again); otherwise step (9) sets `princess = 0` but she **stays aboard**, the jail `IncDays(term)` raises the status to 1, and, unless the ship was sold for the fine, `PrincessReturned` at Galvon still accepts her (kept quirk). The arrest report derives ALR-126 (`PrincessTakenHome`) from `arrested` and the status change. (source: Game.cs:254-271, :2650-2665)
- **QST-PRINCESS-010** {M4} [M][W] Quirk consequence of QST-PRINCESS-009: while she stays aboard after such an arrest, the status counts 1, 2, 3, …; whenever it is 3, a trip to Qonos meets the Scorpion again (ENC-010 (d)), and disabling it sets the status back to 4. (source: Game.cs:807-813, :2650-2665)
- **QST-PRINCESS-011** {M4} [M][W] The escape pod with her aboard sets `princess = 0`, the pod's `IncDays(3)` raises it to 1 while she is still in the old crew (POL-105), and `CreateFlea` removes her: the quest can never be completed (PrincessCentauri and PrincessQonos were removed, PrincessReturned needs her aboard). (source: Game.cs:2066-2070, :2098-2100, :735-741)

## 5. Return and reward

- **QST-PRINCESS-012** {M4} [M][W] `PrincessReturned` (Galvon) is shown iff she is aboard; it is message-only with price 0. Handling removes her from the crew (C# `Ship.Fire`: later slots shift up one; buy prices recalculated if the effective Trader skill changes), sets `princess = 15` and replaces Galvon's event with `PrincessQuantum`. An impatient Princess (status 14) is returned the same way. (source: StarSystem.cs:246-248; Game.cs:2426-2431; Consts.cs:346; Ship.cs:195-213)
- **QST-PRINCESS-013** {M4} [M][W] `PrincessQuantum` is always shown; it is a question with price 0. With no free weapon slot it is rejected with `noFreeSlot` and nothing changes (the event stays for a later visit). Otherwise one Quantum Disruptor is installed, `princess = 16` and the event is removed; the app shows ALR-089 from `specialEventCompleted(.princessQuantum)`. (source: StarSystem.cs:194, :199; Game.cs:2413-2425; Consts.cs:345)
- **QST-PRINCESS-014** {M4} [M][W] Quirk (kept; NEWS-034 lists it faithfully): the arrival headline `princessReturned` is unreachable, because it needs `princess == 15` while Galvon still holds `PrincessReturned`, and the handler that sets 15 replaces that event in the same step. (source: Game.cs:2799-2802, :2426-2431)
- **QST-PRINCESS-015** {M5} [M] Completing the quest (`princess == 16`) selects the Modern "moon-girl" ending at retirement (QST-MOON-012, RUL-006). (source: PRD §4.3)

## 6. Scenarios

- **QST-PRINCESS-016** {M4} [M][W] Success: with `rec = 5`, `rep = 40` at Galvon handle `Princess` (status 1, Galvon event `PrincessReturned`, hidden); Centauri → 2; Inthara → 3; on the trip to Qonos the Scorpion appears at click 1; with a photon disruptor, disable it (status 4); at Qonos accept `PrincessQonos` with a free quarter (aboard, status 4); fly to Galvon (status rising 1 per day); handle `PrincessReturned` (status 15, event `PrincessQuantum`); accept `PrincessQuantum` with a free weapon slot (Quantum Disruptor installed, status 16). (source: Game.cs:2391-2431)
- **QST-PRINCESS-017** {M4} [M][W] Order and gates: with `rec = 4` the start is not offered; at Inthara with status 1 nothing is offered; at Qonos with status 3 nothing is offered (the Scorpion must be disabled first); without disruptors `.attack` on the Scorpion returns `noDisruptors`. (source: StarSystem.cs:230-245; Game.cs:1600-1604)
- **QST-PRINCESS-018** {M4} [M][W] No quarters: at Qonos with status 4 and every crew slot filled, accepting returns `noFreeQuarters` with the state unchanged; after firing a mercenary it succeeds. (source: Game.cs:2400-2405)
- **QST-PRINCESS-019** {M4} [M][W] Impatience: boarding at status 4 and making 5 one-day warps emits `passengerConcerned` on the call that finds status 9; the call that finds 13 emits `passengerImpatient` and zeroes her skills; the status then stays 14 and `PrincessReturned` still completes the quest. (source: Game.cs:2650-2665)
- **QST-PRINCESS-020** {M4} [M][W] Failures: surrendering to pirates with her aboard and no hidden bays → `surrenderPrincess`; an escape pod with her aboard leaves `princess == 1` and she is gone for good; an arrest with two or more mercenaries leaves her status unchanged and the quest stuck; an arrest with fewer and the fine paid keeps her aboard at status 1 (QST-PRINCESS-009). (source: Game.cs:254-271, :1860-1863, :2066-2070)
